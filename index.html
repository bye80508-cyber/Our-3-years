En pondati ❤️ Three years... and still, there are so many little things about you that make my days special. Un kooda pesura simple moments kooda enakku romba precious. Thank you for being part of my story. Innum neraya memories create pannalaam. Happy 3 years, my favourite person. ❤️ — Yours, always cheering for you.
